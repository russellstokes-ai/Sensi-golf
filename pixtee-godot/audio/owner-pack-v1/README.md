Owner-supplied base recordings, 13 October 2026

The source ZIP and all 20 recordings are preserved byte for byte. `manifest.json`
records their SHA-256 hashes. No licence documents accompanied the ZIP.

The single theme plays on the title and pause menus. There is no gameplay music.
Nature clips play individually in random order at 75% base volume, multiplied
by the saved Ambience slider. The first clip starts after 2–6 seconds; a completed
clip is followed by 8–20 seconds of quiet. Track duration therefore determines
the interval between starts. Pausing freezes the clip and its quiet-gap timer.
This scheduling uses a cosmetic RNG independent of native gameplay.

Tree, flagpole, terrain and cup recordings follow confirmed native contacts.
Bird and rock recordings are retained for actual native collisions; importing
them does not implement those detectors. The Eagle recording's classification
and the two cup variants require listening review before final signoff.
Applause, reward and further gameplay sounds are a later owner-supplied pass.

Audio ingestion and playback tests do not certify complete commercial audio,
seamless listening quality, device audio focus or licensing clearance.
