## 1. Explicit decisions that must never be lost

1. **Game viewpoint:** true straight-down Sensible Golf-style overhead 2D. **No tilted/isometric view**. Camera follows the actual ball, shows a close playable slice of a **much larger** hole; do not squeeze tee and green simultaneously into one tall screen or resize player relative to the course. The mini-map may show the whole hole. Uniform world-to-screen X/Y scaling. Lock approved Pixtee golfer/16-bit pixel-art montage quality, course/world scale and portrait phone + Fold closed/open adaptations. Original viewport pixel ratios still need reference measurement; do not call current zoom 1.9 calibrated.
2. **HUD:** hole/par/yards, wind indicator as applicable, mini-map, **target X in the world**, visible ball, club/yardage, the **WHACK-O-METER**, and the **ball-lie graphic** (on tee, fairway, rough, sand, green, etc.). The visible ball's position/height against the lie indicator conveys the quality of contact, as in original game. No explanatory control text inside gameplay: teach interaction in onboarding.
3. **Mobile touch:** holding/touching left of the X rotates aim left gently; right of X rotates right gently; touching club cycles clubs; pressing the same pulsing meter touch area drives all swing presses. Ensure left/right controls cannot accidentally initiate a swing, and aim/club are locked during swing. Onboarding explains; gameplay does not.
4. **Horizontal meter is VISUAL ONLY:** The original meter is semicircular, but the owner **approved horizontal presentation for mobile**. Use one continuous moving indicator: starts at left, traverses towards maximum power on the right, reverses towards the starting/accuracy zone; the player captures power on the outward movement and accuracy on return. The physical horizontal position of the cursor and zones must be derived from the authoritative classic raw meter samples; do not use a generic coloured power slider, a stationary pointer or a symmetric red-centred power bar. **Original red straight-hit region with neighbouring yellow hook/slice regions** must be faithfully remapped to the horizontal display. The exact original asset RGB/color boundaries, rendered zones, pointer geometry and full raw-tick-to-pixel map are **NOT YET VERIFIED**, so DO NOT invent numbers/claim those details are copied exactly. They require original executable/artwork reference verification and approval.
5. **Visual assets:** Individual golfer poses and course tiles require owner approval under [ART_REVIEW_REGISTER.json](ART_REVIEW_REGISTER.json); none of the previous crude programmer-art prototypes is an acceptable production fallback. Sponsor boards are non-colliding world-space scenery, never HUD ads or hazards. Scenery animations have no effect on gameplay RNG/physics/collision.
6. **Content:** 35 original Pixtee courses x 18 playable holes is the current approved art/content target; seeded layouts are NOT the same as 630 production-polished holes. Classic-style modes, 3/9/18 rounds, career/rewards/stats, multiplayer/modes where agreed, persistence and reliable round progression need independent tests. Do not confuse this with the original game's course/resource count.

## 2. Recovered original: authoritative input and timing

- Recovered original gameplay logical timer: `0x3A8` in 16.16 seconds = `65536/936` approximately **70.017 Hz**. Drive physics and swing sampling by a fixed logical clock; rendering at 60/90/120 Hz cannot alter outcomes.
- 13 original club indices, `0..12`; club 12 = putter with separate ground-roll model. 12-bit heading `0..4095`, wraparound with original sign/orientation conventions (not Android degrees).
- Original sample/capture contract: `(club_index, aim_raw, captured_power, accuracy_tick)`, with power range `0..105`, sweet-spot accuracy reading **63**. The original meter rising counter caps at 105; recovered falling branch starts from 70 and transforms its reading. **This is not a standard normalized 0..1 sine meter.**
- Three swing phases: click 1 starts outward motion; click 2 captures current power and starts returning downswing; click 3 captures accuracy and queues **one** shot. No duplicate dispatch. Pause/cancel/rotate/resume cannot create additional shots. Sample at authoritative logical click time, not guessed frame position.
- Verified shot-launch relationships: accuracy error `e = accuracy_tick - 63`; effective power is captured power less absolute error (floor zero); initial heading adjusts by `-16*e` in 12-bit units; selected club/lie profile also changes curvature on successive ball ticks. This is **not** just a one-time angle perturbation. Use verified reference for club/lie-based error windows. **Full extreme accuracy miss handling remains unresolved** in the parity-restored normal-shot launcher.
- The original manual and reviews establish that red is the accurate straight contact zone, adjacent yellow induces curve/slice, and the permissible contact zone tightens for unfavourable lies. **Do not encode unverified exact visual colours, physical tick windows, or power-vs-timing speeds as if proven.** Recover those using the existing disassembly/audits and visual capture.
- Original restoration input adapter: `engine/src/classic_mobile_controls.cpp`, its declaration and tests. Its source explicitly states it handles **click sequencing only**; it expects raw samples from the still-needed recovered original meter scheduler/rendering. Original timer/disassembly references: `.github/workflows/step3-welly-meter.yml` and `.github/workflows/gate1-final-timing-meter.yml`.

## 3. Ball flight, spin, animation and shot result — preservation checklist

- Ball position, height, vertical force, horizontal force and 12-bit direction are separate engine state: do not animate the golfer and ball as though they move together. Impact sprite clubface must coincide with actual ball at launch before separation. Shadow/lift and camera follow driven by the **authoritative simulated ball**.
- Launch depends on selected club, lie profile, captured power and accuracy. The reference includes 13 club rows, a 13x10 lie/profile selector, 11 accuracy/curvature profiles and signed fixed-point/Q14 directional projection. **Never replace with one arbitrary angle offset + sine parabola and call it parity.**
- Per tick: apply original vertical force/gravity to height; resolve touchdown before airborne XY motion where required; horizontal drag and direction/spin correction follow original branch order. When positive horizontal force crosses below zero, **no extra XY movement on that tick**. Preserve original signed shifts, overflow/order semantics in the licensed restoration; independently authored Pixtee implementation must be tested against externally observable traces.
- On ordinary ground contact, vertical rebound reverses with loss; horizontal movement transfers some bounce energy and is damped; further smaller bounces, rolls and rest happen in physics, **not as a fixed-length cosmetic phase**. Bounce count is not artificially limited to one. The camera/ball/shadow must reflect those changing real state variables.
- Ball flight and bounce must respond to club, lie, impact error, draw/fade and the underlying actual collision/surface—no universal 4.5-second roll cutoff or invented flat friction shortcut accepted as 'same as original'.
- Reference tests: `engine/tests/recovered_flight_tests.cpp`, `engine/tests/recovered_ground_tests.cpp`; `spec/pixtee/PHYSICS_BEHAVIOUR_BASELINES.md`. Example classic full-power neutral driver: first ground contact **tick 80**, rest **tick 152** in the controlled original-engine test. This does not establish universal timings on every course or prove current Pixtee parity.

## 4. Lies, collision geometry, bunkers, water, obstacles and terrain

- **Collision must come from authoritative course terrain data**, not visual rough/fairway/river artwork. Original reference uses `MAPM/MAPS/MAPI/SPT` map/descriptor logic with detailed surface subcells and a separate green-mode coordinate transform. Own Pixtee course geometry must maintain rendering/collision alignment while remaining original artwork/layouts.
- Preserve distinctions: tee, ordinary fairway/skirt, semi-rough, rough, very rough, sand/bunkers, green, water, no-go and out-of-bounds. These influence lie, ball presentation, safe strike window, initial shot characteristics, ground behaviour and hazards. The visual ball lie graphic is required—**the same ball should visibly sit proud on a tee or recessed in rough/sand where appropriate**.
- Bunker contact and next-shot lie must be genuine; do not turn a bunker into a mere beige decal. Trees and other obstructing scenery must have real collision only where evidence confirms original comparable obstacle behavior; purely decorative flowers, spectators and paid sponsor boards must never collide. Tree/obstacle exact rules require additional measured reference before fidelity signoff.
- Water, no-go, out-of-bounds: recovered original special hazard code 35 has **immediate movement stop on contact**, distinct from sand/rough. Hazard handling and relief/safe-anchor positioning must preserve original state machine; original-session hazard pause and acknowledgment are not equivalent to silently teleporting the ball to last shot and adding a generic penalty.
- Original course coordinates/map windows and numerical descriptor tables are **not portable Pixtee course assets**. Do not copy original layouts/hazards or tile files without appropriate rights.

## 5. Putting, green slopes, cups and scoring

- Putter index 12 is a genuine **ground** shot with zero ball height. Original green roll uses about **half the drag** of non-green putts; green slope adds frame/tick-consistent XY displacement. Slopes change path and final resting position, not the rendering only. Green/skirt have different roll distance and rest tick for same raw putt.
- Preserve the original green-mode transition and slope-direction input logic, and make display arrows/indicators consistent with simulated break where present. Example tested original putt: same short strength rests at **13 ticks on green** versus **7 on skirt** (controlled test). Exact other test vectors are in `PHYSICS_BEHAVIOUR_BASELINES.md`.
- Cup completion is **not simply ball distance <= arbitrary radius**, nor is the flag sprite itself the cup. Original reference includes separate cup terminal code 8, zero-distance scoring pre-update and some special-green terminal paths. Do not report 'holed' merely because a ball landed anywhere on the green, and do not auto-end 12 strokes as if it were a classic rule without separate evidence.
- Keep distinct: shot stroke registration, putt count (if added), penalties, scored-hole state, terminal pause/acknowledgement, course resource change and next hole. Scoring of an 18-hole route is already reference-tested in original restoration, but this does **not** establish complete human-play acceptance or exact Pixtee round parity.
- **Unresolved:** original code-9/code-10 special-green event-11 post-stop continuation, unsupported non-putter green descriptor branches, some extreme accuracy cases, rare interaction activation. Do not silently invent them.
