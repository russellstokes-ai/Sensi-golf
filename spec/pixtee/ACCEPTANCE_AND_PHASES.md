# Pixtee Golf — independent product gates

## Status

Specification only: NOT a built Android game. Working title pending trademark clearance.

## Phase A — one genuinely playable original hole

Android APK installs, launches without commercial game assets, supports closed-phone and fold-expanded landscape, on-screen 3-click shot, aim, 13 clubs, ball arc/ground bounce/putt, hole scoring and replay. Keyboard/mouse not required. Genuine original Pixtee course geometry and sprites. Dedicated onscreen pause/resume and camera pan/zoom. Capture video and automated shot trace.

## Phase B — experience matching

Measure the observed classic reference and independent Pixtee play side by side for: ball/flag/golfer ratios, view projection/camera tracking, meter travel time and sweet spot, flight/landing, texture colour families and sound pacing. Address significant feel differences without importing any source/asset/program table. Record tester scores and metrics. Finish special terrain (bunker/water/rough/green), course hazards and shot-stage transitions.

## Phase C — content and modes

9/18-hole rounds, 3/9/18-hole quick play, campaign tiers, independent competitions, AI golfer scoring, career save-state, rewards and achievements, detailed stats, accessory unlocks and content persistence/backup.

## Phase D — device, QA and legal

Regression fixtures for all new courses and common strokes, cold boot after process kill, duplicate tap protection, fold open/closed, touchscreen accessibility, no internet requirement, asset provenance complete, game title clearance, copyright/trade-dress review, independent source review, licensing review before any original reuse.

## Explicitly excluded until licensed

Original commercial executable/sprite/sound/course archives; recovered Sensi-golf source or large proprietary numeric tables; recoloured/shifted original course assets; exact source-level recreation under an unverified claim of clean-room development.

## Separate Sensi-golf restoration goal

Sensi-golf is the **genuine original engine restoration** and uses different legal assumptions. Its first Android test may require end-user-supplied original licensed course resources because redistribution permission is not currently established. Sensi-golf test completion does NOT clear Pixtee for commercial publication.
